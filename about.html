About

01. Summary

I'm the kind of person who gets more energy from a busy day than a quiet one - I do my best work around people, build relationships quickly, and I'm genuinely curious about how others are doing. Today, I work as an IT Specialist in GenAI at Arla Foods, where I turn old, repetitive tasks into smart, automated workflows.

I'm driven by a mix of technical depth and steady growth - always looking to master the details while taking on more responsibility along the way. Most of my time is spent working in development environments, building solutions together with tools like Claude.

Before moving into tech, I spent years in more hands-on roles - from assembly work at Camro A/S to serving guests at Bowl'n'fun and baking at Peders Bageri - experience that shaped a strong work ethic long before I started studying mathematics-economics.
